## Learning objectives

By the end of this session you will be able to:

- Present a small empirical study in eight minutes: what you did, what you found, and what you do not know.
- Defend a specific error attribution under questioning.
- Say what would have to be true for your result to generalise beyond your 40 items.

## Agenda

1. **Presentations** — 8 minutes + 4 minutes of Q&A per group. Five slides, one per report section. Every member speaks.
2. **Wrap-up** — what carried across all the tracks, and where the field is going.

Have your `show_errors` table open before you start.

## Mini-project

Final presentations and Q&A. Format and criteria:
[Deliverables](../../final-project/deliverables.md) · [Rubric](../../final-project/rubric.md).

Every group gets these two questions, so prepare them:

1. **"Show us an item the model got wrong, and tell us whose fault it was — the model's or
   your scheme's."**
2. **"What did your QC pass change?"**

Neither is a trick. They are the two questions that separate having run the cells from having
done the work.
